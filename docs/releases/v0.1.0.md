## Whereabouts 0.1.0 — developer preview

Whereabouts shows which app on your Windows PC connects to which server, company and country, and how much it sends, **without ever reading what is sent**. Everything stays on your computer; Whereabouts itself makes no internet connections.

This is a **developer preview**: the first version you can install, meant for people who are happy to try early software and report problems.

### What you get

- A **background service** that starts with Windows and records the outside of every connection (which app, which server, when, how much), never its contents.
- A **desktop app** (no administrator rights needed) with:
  - **Overview**: live connections, top apps, companies and countries, and health checks;
  - **Apps**: each app's publisher, where it connects, and how much it sends;
  - **Connections**: everything live, searchable, with filters for trackers, unverified names and unsigned apps;
  - **Privacy**: exactly what is kept, for how long, and where, with **Export to CSV** and **Delete everything** (each confirmed in a Windows dialog).
- **Who is behind a connection**, looked up offline: DB-IP Lite (country and network owner), EasyPrivacy (tracker domains) and Whereabouts' own company list.
- **Which app is which**: each program's code signature is checked on your computer (no certificate downloads), so a renamed program cannot pretend to be another.
- **Encrypted history** in `C:\ProgramData\Whereabouts`, readable only by administrators and the system: 30 days of connections, then hourly totals for 180 days. Web browsers' history is kept as main domains only.

### Install

1. Download `Whereabouts_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe` and `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
2. **Check the checksum** — this preview is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn:
   `(Get-FileHash .\Whereabouts_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256).Hash`
3. Run it and approve the administrator prompt. It installs to `C:\Program Files\Whereabouts`.

Requirements: Windows 10 22H2 or Windows 11, 64-bit. Uninstall from Settings → Apps; you are asked whether to delete your saved history (default: keep).

### Known limitations

- The installer is **not code-signed** yet (signing is being arranged).
- Connections that started before Whereabouts may have no domain name.
- Browsers using DNS-over-HTTPS may show addresses and network owners instead of domain names.
- No blocking, alerts or notifications yet; those come in 0.2.
